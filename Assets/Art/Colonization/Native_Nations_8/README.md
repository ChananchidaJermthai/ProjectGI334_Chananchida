# Week 6 — Eight Native Nations

This set contains 8 nations, 8 full-body leader PNGs at exactly 512 × 1024, 8 settlement PNGs at exactly 256 × 256, and 8 original game-emblem flags in SVG and PNG. Each nation also has a profile image and data. All production PNG canvases have transparent alpha outside their subject or flag.

## How to use
Open `index.html` to review the eight profile cards, or read `research.md`. Import the `leader_512x1024.png`, `village_256x256.png` and `flag_game_emblem.png` files into Unity as Sprite (2D and UI), Single. Enable Alpha Is Transparency. The JPG profiles are presentation pages, not transparent game sprites. `nations.json` and `nations.csv` hold the game data.

## Research versus game design
These are visual interpretations for a colonial-era game, not authenticated portraits or exact archaeological reconstructions. Facial features, fine decoration and poses are interpretive. Each leader has a separate approximate date; the roster is a cross-period cast and does not claim all eight met or ruled simultaneously. Onondaga is a nation within the Haudenosaunee; Powhatan denotes the chiefdom; Kondiaronk is specifically identified as Tionontati-Wendat.

All eight flags were newly designed for the game, as allowed in the assignment. They are landscape/architecture emblems, not historical national flags, present-day tribal flags or sacred-symbol reproductions. All designs are distinct.

Preferred terrains map regional environments to this project's terrain vocabulary. They are game choices, not a claim that communities lived exclusively in three biomes. Prairie represents cleared agricultural ground in this abstraction, and Swamp includes wetland access. Specialist jobs and the two chosen goods simplify broader economies. Aggressive is an editable AI balance value, never a statement about an ethnic group's character. Rivals refer to specific historical contexts; they are not eternal enemies. See each entry for date qualifications.

The requested visual deliverables are transparent raster PNG assets; only the newly designed flags are also true SVG vectors.

## Contents
Each numbered nation folder: leader PNG, settlement PNG, flag PNG, flag SVG, profile JPG and data JSON. `overview.jpg` shows all eight; `index.html` displays all profile pages.
